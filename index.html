Compliance Alerts
3 active alerts · Regulatory deadlines and risk items
Active Alerts
Child Safety — Background checks not current. Any volunteer working with minors without a current background check represents immediate legal and liability exposure. This is the #1 church liability risk. Complete before allowing any volunteer to serve in children's ministry.
Financial Controls — No segregation of duties. A single person controlling the offering collection, deposit, recording, and reconciliation creates significant fraud risk and will be flagged in any independent audit. Implement a two-person offering count team immediately.
Form 990 — Annual filing deadline approaching. The IRS automatically revokes 501(c)(3) status after 3 consecutive missed filings. Reinstatement requires a new application with fees. File on time or request an extension (Form 8868) before the deadline.
State Charity Registration — Online fundraising triggers multi-state obligations. Soliciting donations via email or website may require registration in states where donors reside — not just where you are incorporated. Review the Charleston Principles and register in all applicable states.
Board Governance — Conflict of interest disclosures due. Annual signed conflict of interest disclosures from all board members are required for IRS 501(c)(3) compliance and should be collected at the first board meeting of each fiscal year.
